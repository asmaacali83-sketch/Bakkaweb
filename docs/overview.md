# Bakka Training Center – Administration System

## 1. Project Overview
Build a secure, responsive web-based administration system for **Bakka Training Center**. The platform manages the training center's daily operations, including users, staff, students, courses, student enrollment, attendance, certificates, and administrative reporting.

The system has three primary account roles:
- **Admin** – manages the entire system and controls access.
- **Staff** – performs the operational tasks explicitly assigned by an admin.
- **Student** – accesses their own profile, enrolled courses, learning information, and certificates.

Each role must have a separate dashboard and permissions. A user must never see or modify information outside their authorized scope.

## 2. Main Objectives
1. Centralize student and staff records.
2. Make student registration and course enrollment straightforward.
3. Manage courses, batches, schedules, and enrollment status.
4. Track student progress and attendance where enabled.
5. Issue verifiable certificates to eligible students.
6. Provide role-specific dashboards and reports.
7. Protect personal data through authentication, authorization, validation, and audit logs.

## 3. Roles and Access Control

### Admin
Admin has system-wide access and can:
- View dashboard statistics and reports.
- Create, edit, deactivate, and manage staff accounts.
- Create, edit, archive, and manage student records.
- Create and manage courses, categories, batches, schedules, and capacity.
- Register students and enroll students in courses.
- Assign staff to courses or batches.
- Manage attendance and student results/progress.
- Approve certificate issuance, issue certificates, revoke certificates with a reason, and verify certificate records.
- Manage system settings, roles, permissions, and audit logs.

Only authorized admins should be able to create another admin or change high-level permissions.

### Staff
Staff access is limited to assigned responsibilities and, where applicable, assigned courses/batches. Staff may:
- View their own profile and assigned work.
- View students in their assigned courses/batches.
- Register a student if the admin grants the `students.create` permission.
- Enroll students if granted `enrollments.create`.
- Record attendance and update progress/results for assigned classes if granted.
- View course and batch details relevant to their assignment.
- Prepare certificate requests if granted, but issuance/approval remains an admin action by default.

Staff must not manage admin accounts, global system settings, permissions, or records outside their assigned scope.

### Student
Students may:
- View and update permitted fields of their own profile.
- View their own enrollment history and current course details.
- View their own schedule, attendance, and progress/results when enabled.
- View, download, and verify their own issued certificates.
- View announcements relevant to their courses.

Students must not view other students' personal records or change enrollment status, grades, attendance, or certificate status.

## 4. Core Modules

### 4.1 Authentication and User Management
- Login and logout.
- Secure password hashing.
- Password reset flow.
- Role-based and permission-based authorization.
- Account activation/deactivation.
- Session/token expiry and refresh strategy.
- Optional email or phone verification.
- Audit log for sensitive actions.

### 4.2 Student Management
- Unique student number generated by the system.
- Personal details: full name, contact details, address, date of birth (if required), and emergency contact (optional).
- Registration date and status.
- Search, filter, sort, and pagination.
- Student profile with enrollment, attendance, progress, and certificate history.
- Avoid collecting unnecessary personal information.

### 4.3 Staff Management
- Staff number, name, contact details, job title, and status.
- Assign staff to courses/batches.
- Configure permissions according to responsibilities.
- View staff activity and assigned workload.

### 4.4 Course Management
- Course title, code, description, category, duration, fee (if applicable), prerequisites, and status.
- Course batches/intakes with start/end dates, capacity, venue or online details, and schedule.
- Assign one or more staff members to a batch.
- Publish/unpublish courses.

### 4.5 Student Registration and Course Enrollment
Keep **student registration** separate from **course enrollment**:
1. Create the student profile.
2. Generate a unique student number.
3. Select an available course and batch.
4. Validate prerequisites, capacity, and duplicate active enrollment.
5. Save enrollment with status such as `pending`, `approved`, `active`, `completed`, `withdrawn`, or `rejected`.
6. Record who created/approved the enrollment and when.

A student may have multiple enrollments over time. Do not overwrite old enrollment history.

### 4.6 Attendance and Progress
- Record attendance by class/session.
- Attendance statuses: present, absent, late, excused.
- Track assessments or course completion requirements if needed.
- Restrict staff updates to their assigned batches.
- Keep a history of changes to attendance and results.

### 4.7 Certificates
- Issue a certificate only when completion requirements are satisfied and an authorized admin approves it.
- Generate a unique certificate number and secure verification code.
- Store student, course, batch, issue date, issuer, and certificate status.
- Provide a printable/downloadable PDF certificate.
- Public verification page should reveal only the minimum information needed to confirm authenticity.
- Support statuses such as `issued`, `revoked`, and `replaced`.
- Record revocation reason and actor; never silently delete an issued certificate.

### 4.8 Dashboard and Reports
**Admin dashboard**
- Total active students and staff.
- Active courses and batches.
- Pending registrations/enrollments.
- Completion and certificate counts.
- Recent activity and operational summaries.

**Staff dashboard**
- Assigned courses/batches.
- Students in assigned batches.
- Upcoming sessions.
- Attendance tasks and pending work.
- Recent relevant activity.

**Student dashboard**
- Personal profile summary.
- Current and past enrollments.
- Course schedule and progress.
- Attendance summary.
- Issued certificates and download/verification links.

Dashboard metrics must be calculated from authorized data, not merely hidden in the UI.

## 5. Suggested Navigation

### Admin
Dashboard · Students · Staff · Courses · Batches · Enrollments · Attendance · Certificates · Reports · Users & Permissions · Audit Logs · Settings

### Staff
Dashboard · My Courses/Batches · My Students · Attendance · Progress/Results · Profile

### Student
Dashboard · My Profile · My Courses · Schedule · Attendance · Progress · My Certificates

The backend must enforce permissions for every route and API endpoint even if a navigation item is hidden.

## 6. Key Workflows

### New student and enrollment
Admin or authorized staff creates a student record → system generates student number → authorized user selects course/batch → system validates capacity and duplicate enrollment → enrollment is saved → authorized approver confirms it if approval is required → student sees the enrollment in their dashboard.

### Course completion and certificate
Staff records attendance and assessment/progress → system evaluates completion rules → eligible student appears in certificate review queue → admin approves issuance → system generates certificate number and verification code → student downloads certificate → verifier can validate it using the public verification page.

## 7. Non-Functional Requirements
- Responsive design for desktop, tablet, and mobile.
- Accessible forms, readable typography, and clear validation messages.
- Secure server-side authorization and input validation.
- Database transactions for multi-step operations.
- Search, filtering, pagination, and useful empty/loading/error states.
- Backups and a documented restore process.
- Environment variables for secrets; never commit credentials.
- Logging that avoids passwords, tokens, or unnecessary personal data.
- Automated tests for authentication, permissions, enrollment, and certificates.

## 8. Acceptance Criteria
- Admin, staff, and student accounts land on their correct dashboards after login.
- Direct API requests cannot bypass role or record-level permissions.
- Staff can only access assigned records and permitted actions.
- Students can only access their own records.
- Duplicate active enrollment in the same batch is prevented.
- Batch capacity and prerequisites are validated on the server.
- Certificate issuance is restricted to eligible students and authorized admins.
- Every sensitive change is attributable to a user and timestamp.
- The application works on mobile and desktop.
